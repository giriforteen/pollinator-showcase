Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

Our Approach

FORTEEN MIND LABS is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe in transparency about what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Core Principle: We collect minimal data. We prioritize your privacy. We never sell your information.

Information We Collect

When you interact with FORTEEN MIND LABS episodes and content, we may collect:

  • Usage Data: Which episodes you view, how long you engage, and which interactions you complete (to improve content quality)
  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, and operating system (for technical optimization)
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated, anonymized patterns of how users engage with our content

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identifying information (unless you explicitly provide it)
  • Email addresses (unless you subscribe to updates)
  • Payment information (all transactions handled by secure third-party processors)
  • Responses to interactive elements (these remain client-side only)

How We Use Information

Any information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Improve episode quality and user experience
  • Understand which patterns resonate with audiences
  • Optimize technical performance across devices
  • Develop new content based on engagement patterns
  • Communicate updates (only if you've opted in)

Third-Party Services

We may use:

  • Analytics Services: To understand aggregated usage patterns (anonymized data only)
  • Hosting Providers: To deliver content securely and reliably
  • Payment Processors: For Clinical Companion Guide purchases (we never see or store payment details)

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We choose partners who share our commitment to privacy.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Unsubscribe from communications at any time
  • Request clarification about our data practices

Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality (e.g., remembering your progress in an episode during a single session). We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data we collect. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use secure connections when accessing our content.

Children's Privacy

Our content is designed for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18. If you believe we've inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our platform evolves. Significant changes will be communicated clearly. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
